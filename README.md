I help organizations reduce risk fast: align security strategy to business goals, build pragmatic security programs that satisfy regulators and boards, and ship security engineering that scales. I've led security for a regulated payments fintech and a global logistics-software company, and I run **LufSec**, where I've trained tens of thousands of practitioners in offensive, hardware, and AI security.

## What I do

- **Security leadership:** strategy, risk management, governance, board & audit-committee reporting, security operating model, M&A due diligence, vendor risk
- **Regulated-industry programs:** PCI DSS, SOC 2, ISO 27001, fintech/payments compliance, AI governance committees
- **AI / LLM security & governance:** GenAI risk assessment, red-teaming LLM applications, prompt-injection & jailbreak testing, guardrails, responsible-AI governance (C|RAGE), AI acceptable-use and adoption policy
- **Security engineering:** detection & response, cloud security, identity, AppSec, automation and tooling
- **Offensive & hardware security:** penetration testing, IoT/ICS reconnaissance, firmware analysis, automotive/CAN-bus research
